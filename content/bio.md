Hi, I am Qiuhao Zhao (Brad). I am a PhD candidate at the [School of Earth Sciences, Zhejiang University](http://gs.zju.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Pingbin Jin](https://person.zju.edu.cn/0091318). Prior to this, I received my B.S. in Human Geography and Urban-Rural Planning from Zhejiang University in 2022.

My research sits at the intersection of **urban environment and mental health**, **urban sustainability and social equity**, and **tourism big data**. I use spatiotemporal machine learning, multimodal data, and geographic information science to understand how people experience places and how those experiences vary across communities.

My current project, [**How Cities Become Musical**](/music-geography/), develops a multimodal and spatiotemporal framework for studying how music, audiovisual representation and platform reception selectively recognise urban places and communities.
